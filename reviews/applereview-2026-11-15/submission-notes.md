# Tasquel 1.1 (12): App Store Connect text

Paste into App Store Connect yourself. It states only what the code and this review confirmed. Update it if a fix changes what it says.

## What's New in This Version

- Days for tasks: give a task a day of the week in its options, or type "on Friday" in Smart Add. On its day it reads Today, and once the day has gone by unfinished it's marked late. Tap a day at the top to see just that day's tasks. A new task for a day that's already gone by this week goes into next week.
- Dates and reminders: give a task a date, its last day. A one-time task moves into that week, and a carry-over task comes along until then. A notification reminds you at 9 AM the day before. Smart Add spots dates you type and offers to set them.
- Pins: keep up to two cards first in the grid, and up to two tasks at the top of a card.
- Progress rings: a card's ring fills as you go, counting each step of a goal and each item of a list.
- Duplicates flagged: tasks or list items with the same name in one card are marked.
- Seasonal look: Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Seasonal changes Tasquel's colors with the season, and the logo dresses up for the holidays.
- Back to This Week: on another week, tap the green house in the bottom bar. Go to Week rings the days your tasks are dated or set for.
- Faster: scrolling, opening cards and checking off tasks are much quicker, even with lots of tasks.
- Help is now in tabs, starting with answers to common questions.
- Also: add several categories in one go, clear a name and press Return to delete it, and fixes for VoiceOver, iPad and smaller iPhones.

## Notes for the Reviewer (App Review Information ▸ Notes)

Tasquel needs no account or sign-in, and everything is stored on the device.

To try what's new in 1.1:
1. Tap a category card to open it.
2. Add a task with the + at the bottom of the card, then tap the task's type and mode button (the small icons at its right). The Day row picks a day of the week; the calendar at the end of that row picks a date.
3. A new task given a day that has already passed this week goes into next week, and the screen follows it there. Tap Back to This Week, or the green house in the bottom bar, to return.
4. Tap Edit at the bottom right to pin up to two cards (the pin at a card's corner) or add categories. A card's own edit button (the pencil) pins tasks.
5. Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Seasonal shows the seasonal colors.

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled on the device. Tasquel asks for notification permission only the first time a task is given a date, or when Settings ▸ Reminders ▸ Expiration Reminders is turned on; it never asks at launch. Without permission, everything else works the same.

Smart Add (the sparkles button in a card's Add Task field) uses Apple Intelligence on the device. On a device without Apple Intelligence, or with it turned off, the button isn't shown and tasks are added exactly as typed. Everything else works the same.

Calendar events are optional. Tasquel asks for calendar access only when you turn them on, and it only reads events.

The only data the app sends is the optional, anonymous Feature Ideas survey in Settings. It goes to jbfentner.com, and only when the user taps Send Anonymously: the features ticked, an optional comment and the app version. Support and Privacy in Settings open web pages.
